Frequently asked questions

What should I do if I smell gas at home?

Close the cylinder or main gas valve, open windows, avoid switching electrical items on or off, and call a technician. Do not light a flame until the leak is fixed.

Do you do gas pipeline work for shops and restaurants?

Yes. We fit gas pipelines for both homes and commercial kitchens in Kandivali and nearby areas.

Do you test for leaks after installation?

Every pipeline job ends with leakage testing at all joints, valves and appliance connections.
