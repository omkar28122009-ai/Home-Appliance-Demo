Frequently asked questions

Why does my hob burner switch off after I release the knob?

This usually points to the flame failure safety device (thermocouple). It can be cleaned, realigned or replaced by a technician.

Can you fit a new hob in my existing kitchen platform?

Yes. We check the cut-out size, adjust where possible and install the hob with proper gas connection and leakage testing.

Do you repair all hob brands?

We service most common built-in hob brands used in Mumbai homes. Share the brand and model when booking so the technician comes prepared.
