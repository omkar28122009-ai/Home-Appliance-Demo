Frequently asked questions

How often should RO filters be changed?

Pre-filters and carbon filters are usually changed every 6 to 12 months and the RO membrane every 1 to 2 years, depending on water quality and usage.

Why is my purifier producing very little water?

Clogged filters, a worn membrane, low inlet pressure or a faulty pump are the usual causes. A service visit identifies which one.
